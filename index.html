About me

A business leader who speaks technology fluently

I've spent my career at the point where business strategy meets technology. My work starts with the customer journey: understanding where it breaks down, then using technology to grow top-line revenue or take cost out of operations.

Three times, an organization created a new role for me to lead: digital commerce at a national restaurant brand, then digital and later enterprise AI at a regional insurer. Each time the job was to build something that didn't exist yet and prove it was worth the investment.

I lead as a servant leader. I set strategic direction for cross-functional teams, and I stay hands-on with them through execution and delivery. Most recently I built an insurer's enterprise AI strategy, governance, and adoption program from the ground up, putting people and trust ahead of tools.
